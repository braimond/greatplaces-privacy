Frequently asked questions

The app doesn't show any places near me.

  • Make sure you've granted location permission: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Great-Places → While Using the App.
  • Great-Places only shows venues rated 4.0 stars or higher. If you're in an area with few reviews, try increasing the search radius (up to 5km) or switching the category filter to "All".
  • Check that you have an active internet connection — the app needs to query Google's Places API.

How do I change the search radius or category?

Tap the filter icon at the top of the list to open the filter sheet. You can pick a radius (500m, 1km, 2km, or 5km) and a category (All, Restaurants, Cafes, Bars, Hotels, or Shopping).

How is "top-rated" defined?

The app only displays places with a Google rating of 4.0 stars or higher, sorted highest rating first. Ratings come from Google's Places API and reflect real user reviews on Google.

Can I save favorites or read full reviews?

Not in this version. Tap any place card to open it in the Google Maps app, where you can read full reviews, save it, share it, or get directions.

Does Great-Places work outside my home country?

Yes. The app works anywhere Google's Places API has coverage — which is nearly worldwide. Just enable location permission and search nearby.

What data does the app collect?

Only your device location — used solely to find nearby places. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Full details in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Because no personal data is stored on any server, simply uninstalling the app removes everything. Your locally saved filter preferences are removed automatically.
